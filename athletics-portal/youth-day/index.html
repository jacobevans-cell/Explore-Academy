Bring your people.
These tickets are not limited to players or immediate family. Reserve the number your group needs while our allotment lasts.
Why go?
Watching high-level volleyball helps players see the speed, communication, movement, systems, and decision-making we work toward in practice.

A partnership worth using

Through Coach Evans’ volleyball company, Geared for Volleyball, we regularly partner with Grand Canyon University and Arizona State University Volleyball. Those relationships can include complimentary match tickets and, at times, special experiences such as private trainings, team events, and lock-ins. Each year, Coach Evans shares those opportunities with Explore Academy athletes whenever possible.

Why these matches matter

See the next levelCollege volleyball makes court movement, serving pressure, defensive systems, transitions, and communication much easier to understand.
Learn by watchingPlayers begin noticing where athletes move before the ball arrives, how they communicate, and how quickly they reset between plays.
Grow the gameThe more quality volleyball our athletes watch, the more volleyball IQ they bring back to our gym.
